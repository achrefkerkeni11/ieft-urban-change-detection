# Remote-sensing research models

The maintained deliverable in this repository is the final IEFT/LEVIR-CD urban
change-detection pipeline. Its setup, data layout, exact 118k training protocol,
full-scene evaluation, final metrics, and artifact hashes are documented in
[IEFT/IEFT/README.md](IEFT/IEFT/README.md).

`CUP/` and `PERSVL/` are retained research baselines. They are independent of
the final IEFT result and are not imported by its training or evaluation code.
